Free Street Litter Sampler v1.1
by AssetsFactory

14 free realistic street litter: a can, a bottle and a coffee cup in 3 states, plus scatter lots.
Real-world scale (metres), no brands: every label is invented.

These objects come from the Packaging & Litter Kit: 264 packaging and litter objects in 12 families
(cardboard, takeout, cans, bottles, jugs, bags, paper, pallets, tyres and scrap, small debris, bins,
ready-made piles), most in clean, dirty and crushed states:
Search "Packaging & Litter Kit" by AssetsFactory on Fab.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  blender/   Free_Street_Litter_Sampler.blend - Blender asset library (Blender 5.2), procedural PBR materials.
  game/      FBX, GLB and OBJ of every object with baked PBR textures (base colour + alpha, roughness,
             metallic, normal).

USE THE BLENDER ASSET LIBRARY
  Edit > Preferences > File Paths > Asset Libraries > "+" and pick the "blender" folder,
  then drag the objects in from the Asset Browser.

PIVOTS
  Centre of the underside, resting on the floor (z = 0). Crushed objects rest on their side.

GAME ENGINES
  GLB has no collision mesh. FBX and OBJ reference the shared
  "textures" folder: keep the folder structure. Clear plastic and glass use alpha blending.
  Tested: re-import in Blender 5.2, glTF validator, Godot 4.7 import. Not tested: Unity, Unreal Engine.

HOW IT WAS MADE
  All meshes and textures are generated by our own Python scripts in Blender. No image or 3D
  generator was used. The scripts were written with the help of an AI coding assistant.
